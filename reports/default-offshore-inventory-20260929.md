# Default offshore display inventory — 2026-09-29

The first-load layer is now a reviewed `key` tier. It is independent of source authority and independent of the deduplication identity.

## Display contract

- `key`: visible by default; regional landmarks users are likely to recognize or navigate by.
- `expanded`: available through “More offshore spots”; not shown at first load.
- `reference`: nearshore/coastal and unresolved records; available only through “All structures” or search.

The current reviewed default contains 27 display entries:

Axel Carlson Reef; Manasquan Ridge; Sandy Hook Reef; Barnegat Ridge South; Barnegat Ridge North; Baltimore Canyon; Poor Man's Canyon; Sausages; The Cigar; Wilmington Canyon; Carteret Canyon; Hudson Canyon; Lindel Cone Canyon; Spencer Canyon; Toms; 21 Mile Hill; 26 Mile (Big) Hill or Lump; Winter Quarter Shoal; Chincoteague Shoal; Romer Shoal; Sea Bright Ground; Lightship Ridge; Miah Maull Shoal; Offshore Southeast; Mako; Norfolk Canyon; Offshore Lump.

Washington Canyon was added to the key inventory in the production follow-up. Source provenance remains raw metadata for audit/details only; it no longer creates an official/unofficial display classification or filter.

## Negative controls

- Susquehanna Flats: not default-visible; unresolved setting.
- Barnegat Inlet Buoy / BI: not default-visible; nearshore/reference.
- Rock Pile / Rock Piles: not default-visible; expanded catalog.
- HA Bouy: not default-visible; nearshore/reference.
