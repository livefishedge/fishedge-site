About FishEdge Live

Finding the frontlines of the bite.

FishEdge started with one angler's frustration: burning fuel to find a bite that was there yesterday and gone today. We combine live satellite oceanography, current models, and chlorophyll to map the ocean's actual edges — temperature breaks, color lines, current rips, Gulf Stream position — into one current view, with no stale filler.

Our Mission

At fishedge.live, our mission is to eliminate the guesswork from offshore and coastal angling by delivering real-time, actionable data on underwater marine edges. We track the critical boundaries where ocean water shifts — giving captains, sport fishers, and local anglers the exact coordinates where predatory fish hunt.

The Ocean Is Vast, But the Bite Is Specific

Every experienced captain knows that fish don't gather randomly in open water. They track the edges:

  • Sudden sea surface temperature breaks
  • Shifting chlorophyll boundaries and color lines
  • Powerful oceanic current rips and upwellings

Finding these invisible boundaries by burning fuel and staring at a basic sonar screen is inefficient, expensive, and stressful. When marine conditions change by the hour, yesterday's hot spot is today's dead water.

Live Edge Intelligence

fishedge.live was built to map these rapidly changing dynamics into a single, user-friendly live interface. We process vast volumes of satellite oceanography, marine altimetry, and weather patterns to pinpoint the few meaningful edges where marine environments collide — so captains get the signal without the overload.

By tracking these active "frontlines," we give you the ultimate edge before you even leave the dock.
